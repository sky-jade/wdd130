My Favourite City

Edinburgh is my favourite city because I love its beautiful scenery, historic buildings, and cosy atmosphere. It feels magical and full of character.

Edinburgh is a beautiful and historic city in Scotland, famous for its stunning Edinburgh Castle, medieval Old Town, Georgian New Town, and Arthur’s Seat, an ancient volcanic hill with amazing views of the city. It also has a fascinating spooky side, with old graveyards, underground spaces, and plenty of ghost stories. People should visit Edinburgh because it offers a mix of **history, culture, nature, adventure, and entertainment** all in one place. You can explore castles and historic streets, hike up Arthur’s Seat, visit museums, enjoy festivals and performances, and experience the city’s unique architecture and atmosphere.
