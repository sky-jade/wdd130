CrossRiver, Nigeria

Cross River State is a beautiful state in southeastern Nigeria, known for its rich culture, natural scenery, and tourist attractions. Its capital is Calabar, a city famous for its clean environment and annual Calabar Carnival. The state is home to places such as the Cross River National Park, Obudu Mountain Resort, and the Kwa Falls. Cross River is also known for its diverse ethnic groups, traditional festivals, and agricultural activities.
